"SwissTools" is fine but a bit generic. A few alternatives off the top of my head:                                    
  - Toolbox — simple, universal, but very taken                                                                           - Quickset — fast set of tools, clean
  - Toolchest — a bit more character
  - Wrenchbox — playful
  - Tinker — catchy, implies hands-on
  - Workbench — developer connotation, also taken everywhere
  - Devkit — dev-focused, a bit serious
  - Nibble — small but useful, different vibe
  - Sidekick — helper tools

  My honest pick: Tinker — short, memorable, implies you're doing small hands-on tasks, and has personality without
  being try-hard. Alternatively if you want something more "utility belt" feel, Toolchest works well.
